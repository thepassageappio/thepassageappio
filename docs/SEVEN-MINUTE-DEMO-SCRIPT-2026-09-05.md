# Passage Authority seven-minute demo script

**Audience:** deposit operations, member/customer operations, compliance operations, legal operations, and digital servicing leaders  
**Environment:** `https://demo.thepassageapp.io`  
**Rule:** use fictional participants and approved sample files only

## Before the meeting

- Sign in with the allowlisted Passage Google account.
- Prepare one fresh Demo request without sending it.
- Keep separate owner, account-holder, representative, and reviewer browser profiles ready.
- Confirm both controlled inboxes are open and the two approved fictional PDFs are available.
- Keep one completed receipt open as backup.

## 0:00–0:40 — Explain the problem

**Show:** Homepage.

**Say:** “When someone needs to help a parent or grandparent under a power of attorney, the request can move through branches, inboxes, documents, and review teams without one clear current record. Passage keeps that request moving from the people involved to the institution's decision.”

**Boundary:** “Passage does not approve the POA, verify identity by itself, grant account access, or move money. The institution keeps those responsibilities.”

## 0:40–1:30 — Institution starts the request

**Show:** Owner workspace and prepared sample request.

**Say:** “The institution identifies both people, the account relationship, the requested actions, and the end date. Nothing is sent until staff review and activate it.”

**Point out:** organization, owner role, evaluation usage, account boundary, two requested actions, and distinct participant email addresses.

**Action:** Activate the request.

## 1:30–2:15 — Account holder confirms

**Show:** Account-holder inbox and secure request.

**Say:** “The account holder receives a private, expiring link and acts first. The representative cannot proceed until this person confirms the names, account boundary, and requested actions.”

**Action:** Open the newest email, start secure access, review, acknowledge, and confirm.

## 2:15–3:25 — Representative accepts and supplies evidence

**Show:** Representative inbox and secure request.

**Say:** “The representative receives a different link. They see only their responsibility and the specific actions being requested.”

**Action:** Accept the responsibility, upload `fictional-poa.pdf`, save the certification, and upload `fictional-identity.pdf`.

**Point out:** each requirement's purpose, private-file message, and the separation between evidence collection and the institution's final decision.

## 3:25–4:20 — Institution reviews

**Show:** Reviewer or institution workspace.

**Say:** “Authorized staff see what is complete and what still needs review. They can accept an item for this review or ask a focused question without restarting the request.”

**Action:** Review both sample files and mark the required steps complete.

## 4:20–5:00 — Representative sends the completed request

**Show:** Representative disclosure.

**Say:** “Before anything is sent for a decision, the representative sees exactly what the institution will receive.”

**Action:** Review the disclosure, acknowledge it, and send the request.

## 5:00–6:00 — Institution records its decision

**Show:** Decision form.

**Say:** “The institution—not Passage—records whether it accepts, limits, or rejects the request.”

**Action:** Choose **Accept with limits**, keep the two requested actions, record two plain-language limits, confirm, and save.

## 6:00–7:00 — Show the completed transaction

**Show:** Decision receipt.

**Say:** “This is the completed transaction. It is not a payment or money movement. It is the institution's saved answer about who may help, for which account relationship, with which actions and limits, and until when.”

**Point out:** receipt code, outcome, people, account boundary, accepted actions, recorded limits, decision date, current status, and revocation/expiration section.

**Close:** “The pilot measures whether this reduces elapsed time, handoffs, repeated information requests, reviewer effort, and inconsistent outcomes in one narrow workflow.”

## Recovery lines

- **Email delay:** “The request records provider status and staff can issue a fresh link without losing saved work.”
- **Used or replaced link:** “Earlier links fail closed and tell the person to use the newest email.”
- **Demo interruption:** Open the prepared completed receipt and continue from the 6:00 section.
- **Identity question:** “Passage coordinates the institution's chosen identity step; the institution determines what proof is required and whether it is sufficient.”
- **Legal-validity question:** “Passage records the review and result. It does not provide a legal opinion or guarantee recognition.”

## Presenter pass criteria

- Seven minutes or less without developer or database access
- All four roles use independent sessions
- Every transition states the current status and next action
- Both invitation emails and both receipt emails arrive in controlled inboxes
- The final receipt agrees across institution, account-holder, and representative views
- No unsupported security, legal, integration, or enterprise-readiness claim is made
