# Passage Authority sales and outbound launch plan

**Status:** Ready for controlled discovery outreach and live synthetic demos  
**Updated:** September 5, 2026

## Single message source

Passage Authority is a shared checklist and decision receipt for a financial institution's power-of-attorney process. It gathers the right information from the account holder and representative, gives institution staff one place to review it, and makes sure everyone sees the same current answer. Passage organizes the workflow; the institution decides whether to recognize the authority.

Do not claim that Passage validates legal authority, replaces identity verification, approves a POA, moves money, or is production-enterprise ready. Say that Passage coordinates identity evidence, authority evidence, institutional review, scope, limits, receipts, and later changes.

## Initial outbound motion

1. Build a first list of 20 New York regional banks and credit unions, with one likely operations buyer and one compliance/legal influencer per account.
2. Lead with the operational problem: POA requests crossing branches, inboxes, documents, and review teams without one current record.
3. Ask for a 20-minute workflow interview, not a purchase. Offer the seven-minute synthetic demo when the problem is confirmed.
4. Qualify on request volume, elapsed time, handoffs, missing-information loops, reviewer effort, complaints, systems touched, and executive ownership.
5. Offer the $5,000, 60–90 day founding pilot only after agreeing on a narrow team, data boundary, success thresholds, and security review path.

## Immediate launch order

1. While independent QA runs, triage every finding into critical, high, medium or low and record it in the demo checklist. Any open critical or high finding stops live demos.
2. Give the one-pager, seven-minute script and three-touch sequence one buyer/sender review; update these source files before changing downstream copy.
3. Build the 20-account New York list, then personalize only the first five accounts around one evidenced operating pain and one discovery question.
4. Connect the Passage HubSpot private app and apply the approved additive, no-delete schema before loading reviewed contacts. Keep all participant and authority-request data out of HubSpot.
5. Start the five-account batch once the timed demo passes. Use early calls to test problem, buyer, urgency and pilot fit—not to claim production-enterprise readiness.

## Stop/go rules

**Go now:** controlled prospect research, discovery outreach, meetings, synthetic demos, and design-partner conversations.

**Do not promise yet:** production customer data, automated legal validation, SSO/SCIM, broad core integration, published annual pricing, or full enterprise readiness.

**Before a controlled real-data pilot:** close privileged MFA, retention/restore/incident evidence, legal/privacy review, scoped security assessment, remaining hosted negative paths, and provider reconciliation.
