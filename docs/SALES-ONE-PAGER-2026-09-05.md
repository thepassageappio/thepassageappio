# Passage Authority

## A clear path for financial power-of-attorney requests

Power-of-attorney requests often cross branches, inboxes, document folders, and review teams. The account holder and representative may not know what is missing, while staff reconstruct the current status from multiple systems.

Passage Authority gives a financial institution one guided process to collect the right information, review it under its own rules, record a decision and limits, and share one current result with the people involved.

### The simple version

Passage is a shared checklist and decision receipt for a financial institution's power-of-attorney process. It gets the right information from each person, lets the institution decide, and makes sure everyone sees the same answer.

### How it works

1. **The institution starts the request.** Staff identify the account holder, representative, account relationship, and requested actions.
2. **Each person completes a private step.** The account holder confirms first. The representative then accepts the responsibility and supplies the required information.
3. **The institution performs its checks.** Authorized staff apply the institution's identity, document, fraud, legal, and policy requirements.
4. **Everyone receives a clear result.** The institution accepts, limits, or rejects the request. Passage records a readable receipt and preserves later changes.

### What the institution gains

- One current request instead of disconnected status updates
- Clear next actions for participants and staff
- Separate identity evidence, authority evidence, and institution decision
- Explicit account scope, permitted actions, limits, and end date
- Role-based access and a saved activity history
- A decision receipt that can remain current after revocation or expiration

### What Passage does not do

Passage does not create or notarize a power of attorney, declare a document legally valid, replace the institution's identity or fraud controls, grant account credentials, approve transactions, or move money. The institution keeps the final decision.

### Initial use case

Passage is starting with New York financial power of attorney for deposit-account servicing. A strong first partner is a regional bank or credit union with visible delays, repeated information requests, inconsistent branch handling, or manual back-office coordination.
