# Passage Authority: next steps to a first sale

## Deal snapshot

This is a first-sale plan for Passage Authority, based on the owner's direction and the [released product evidence](RELEASE-AND-DEMO-STATUS-2026-09-11.md). No customer commitment, opportunity stage or close date is established here. Run mode: Next Actions Only. Product and commercial files supply the evidence; this is not a CRM, customer-call or inbox audit.

The initial audience is a bank or credit union's operations and servicing team. The supported evaluation uses fictional people and documents in the existing New York scenario for statement copies and account questions. The current offer is a $5,000 founding pilot lasting 60–90 days, covering one institution, one team and one workflow, credited toward year one under agreement. Real customer data requires separate agreement and readiness checks. See the [commercial playbook](agent/COMMERCIAL.md).

## Evidence gaps

The application release is verified, but that alone does not establish a reliable fresh demo or a complete institutional product. The immediate demo risks are invitation arrival and the uncompleted timed walkthrough. Policy publication, recovery/restore and qualified reviews are separate remaining work; they cannot be marked complete by changing copy or obtaining another green build.

No buyer interviews, willingness to pay, handling-time baseline, named buying committee or procurement acceptance are established by this checkpoint. The first discovery conversations should collect those facts before making savings, ROI or customer-readiness promises.

The seven-day internal check can reach day seven no earlier than September 15 UTC if all remaining days are clean. This is one calendar requirement, not a promised sales-ready date. The next concrete deliverable is the fresh-demo evidence in action 1.
