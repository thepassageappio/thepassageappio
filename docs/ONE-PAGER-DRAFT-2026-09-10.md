# Passage Authority

## A clearer path from a power-of-attorney request to your institution's decision

When someone asks to act for an account holder, the institution needs a clear request, supporting information, a defined scope and a record of what it decided. Passage Authority brings those steps into one guided workflow.

### One request. Separate responsibilities. A shared record.

1. **Prepare the request.** Identify the people, account relationship and requested actions.
2. **Collect each participant's input.** The account holder and representative complete their own steps.
3. **Review and resolve questions.** The institution reviews supporting evidence, asks for corrections and records its decision.
4. **Keep the outcome clear.** A shared receipt records the decision, accepted scope and any limits.

The institution retains its legal, identity, fraud and account-access decisions. Passage records the workflow and outcome; the institution carries out any resulting account changes through its own systems.

### Start with a focused evaluation

The current evaluation uses fictional people and sample documents for a limited New York scenario: receiving duplicate statements and discussing service issues. It is intended to help an institution assess the workflow before considering a separately approved pilot with customer data.

We are seeking operations and servicing teams at banks and credit unions to help evaluate where requests stall, which evidence matters, and what a clear decision record needs to contain.

**Conversation starter:** How does your team handle these requests today, and where does the process create the most rework?
