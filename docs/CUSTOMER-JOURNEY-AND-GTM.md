# Passage Authority prospect-to-pilot journey

## Positioning

Passage Authority helps a financial institution move a power of attorney request from intake to a clear, limited, and current decision. It is simple for account holders and representatives, auditable for the institution, and designed to connect to existing servicing systems. The institution keeps the final legal and operational decision.

The first wedge is New York financial POA servicing for regional banks and credit unions. No second use case enters build priority until this journey is independently repeatable, demo ready, and accepted by early design partners.

The role and onboarding model is fixed in [Product vision and onboarding](./PRODUCT-VISION-AND-ONBOARDING.md). Institution teams use persistent role-based workspaces; account holders and representatives use request-specific secure links without accounts.

## Customer journey

| Stage | Prospect question | Experience | Success measure | CRM event later |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Is this our POA operations problem? | Specific problem page, plain-language outcome, honest boundary | Qualified visitor can explain Passage in one sentence | Qualified website conversion |
| Evaluate | Is it easier than our current process? | No-card sample workspace and guided request | First request sent in under five minutes without help | Evaluation started |
| Experience | Does it work for every person? | Four-person synthetic journey and matching receipt | Full story completed with zero critical or high defects | Demo completed |
| Validate | Can we trust and integrate it? | Security boundary, control evidence, sandbox quickstart, signed webhook example | Security and technical owners identify no unowned blocker | Technical validation complete |
| Pilot | Can it improve one real workflow safely? | $5,000, 60–90 day proof of concept with agreed scope and measures | Written pilot plan, named owners, approved data boundary, and exit criteria | Pilot committed |
| Convert | Is the operational value worth scaling? | Executive readout using cycle time, handoffs, missing information, consistency, and receipt accuracy | Institution approves production plan and commercial terms | Customer / expansion |

## “Wow” moments to design and test

1. The buyer understands the product before scrolling: one request, one institution decision, everyone current.
2. The owner creates a sample workspace without a card and sends the first request in under five minutes.
3. Participants open a secure link without creating an account and always know what to do next.
4. The reviewer sees missing information and accepted limits without reading a long activity log.
5. Every role opens the same decision receipt, then sees revocation without erasing the original decision.
6. A technical buyer sees a hosted-first path plus a small, testable API and signed-webhook quickstart.

## ICP hypothesis to validate

- Regional bank or credit union with meaningful deposit-account servicing volume.
- POA intake is handled through email, branch handoffs, shared drives, tickets, or inconsistent checklists.
- A senior operations, deposit servicing, compliance, legal-operations, or digital-servicing leader owns the pain.
- The institution can sponsor a narrow New York workflow and name an operations owner plus a security/technology reviewer.
- The buyer values cycle time, fewer handoffs, consistent decisions, complete records, and current downstream status.

Disqualify organizations seeking automated legal opinions, universal document acceptance, immediate core replacement, consumer money movement, or unsupported production certification.

## Pilot offer

- $5,000 founding proof of concept, credited toward year one if converted.
- One institution workflow, one named team, one approved data boundary, 60–90 days.
- Success measures agreed before configuration: completion rate, elapsed time, handoffs, missing-information loops, reviewer effort, decision consistency, receipt accuracy, and support burden.
- Sales-assisted invoice first; no consumer-style checkout and no charge to account holders or representatives.

## CRM design gate

HubSpot should mirror this journey only after the copy, demo, UAT, trust, and integration gates pass. The minimum later schema is organization, institution type, asset/servicing band, geography, current POA process, estimated monthly volume, pain owner, executive sponsor, technical reviewer, stage, next step, demo result, security status, pilot criteria, expected decision date, and loss reason. Automations must follow observed customer behavior; they should not define the product journey before it is validated.
