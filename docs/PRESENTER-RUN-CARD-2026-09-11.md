# Passage presenter run card

**Internal rehearsal guide. The fresh buyer demo has not passed yet.**

Say: “Passage helps a bank or credit union collect a request, review the documents and record a clear decision.”

This example uses made-up people and documents. It covers statement copies and account questions in the supported New York example. The bank decides what to accept. A saved decision does not change access to an account or move money.

## Get ready before starting the clock

1. Use the [Demo site](https://demo.thepassageapp.io). [Sign in to your existing workspace](https://demo.thepassageapp.io/start?intent=sign-in). Complete your normal security check.
2. Open separate browser profiles for the owner, administrator, reviewer, account holder and representative. Check the name and role in each institution profile. Do not reuse a closed test account.
3. Use the two participant email addresses supplied by the owner. The owner approved rehearsal invitations and receipts; the addresses are still pending. Do not guess them.
4. Download the [sample authority document](https://demo.thepassageapp.io/samples/fictional-poa.pdf) and [sample identity document](https://demo.thepassageapp.io/samples/fictional-identity.pdf). Keep both ready to upload. Use no real documents.
5. In the approved presenter workspace, open the demo preparation guide. Choose **Prepare a fresh demo**. This creates a draft; it does not send invitations. Check the fictional details and intended email addresses before sending.

The [read-only example](https://demo.thepassageapp.io/sample) asks signed-out visitors to sign in. It is separate from creating and completing a fresh request.

## Practice the seven-minute story

| Time | Person | Action and visible proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:45 | Presenter | Explain the request and show the different institution roles. |
| 0:45–1:30 | Owner | Review the fresh draft and send it to the approved participants. Show the next step. |
| 1:30–2:15 | Account holder | Open the invitation from the actual inbox in their own profile. Check the people and requested actions, then confirm. |
| 2:15–3:30 | Representative | Open their own invitation, accept and upload both sample files. Follow the page's next step. |
| 3:30–4:15 | Reviewer | Inspect the documents and ask one specific question or request one correction. |
| 4:15–5:00 | Representative | Answer or make the correction, complete the required confirmation and send for review. |
| 5:00–6:00 | Reviewer | Record acceptance with limits. Show exactly which actions were accepted. |
| 6:00–7:00 | All three request parties | Compare the receipt code, decision, actions and limits. Record revocation through the permitted person and check that all three views show the change. |

The times are targets, not measured results. Follow the saved state and visible instructions if a step takes longer.

## Stop and record the issue if

- An invitation cannot be found in the actual inbox. Check the address and spam folder; record arrival time if it appears. Recovering a link from a database or provider dashboard does not count as a passed rehearsal.
- The wrong person can act, a document will not upload, a step loses saved work, or receipts disagree.
- A page needs developer help, the wrong data appears, or an unexplained error occurs.

Never send again just because the first response was slow. Check the page's current state and message history first. Use the provided resend action only when needed and record that recovery separately.
